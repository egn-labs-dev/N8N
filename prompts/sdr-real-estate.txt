TASK: Analyze the following website content and produce a structured analysis + hyper-personalized cold email.

=== ANALYSIS INSTRUCTIONS ===

1. COMPANY NAME: Extract the official company name from the website content.
2. INDUSTRY: Categorize precisely (e.g., "Residential Real Estate Agency", "Commercial Property Management", "Construction & Development", "Real Estate Marketing").
3. MAIN SERVICE: Identify their primary offering (e.g., "Selling luxury apartments in Kyiv", "Property management for landlords").
4. PAIN POINT: Based on what you see on their site, infer their biggest operational pain. Common ones:
   - Slow response time to incoming leads (they lose deals to faster competitors)
   - Manual listing creation and updates across multiple platforms
   - No systematic follow-up with past clients or cold leads
   - Time wasted on repetitive client questions (pricing, availability, specs)
   - Manual data entry from inquiries into CRM/spreadsheets
   - No personalization in outreach to potential sellers/buyers
5. CONTACT EMAIL: Extract any email address found on the page. If none found, say "Not found".

=== COLD EMAIL INSTRUCTIONS ===

Write a SHORT cold email (max 150 words) following this EXACT structure:

1. HOOK (1 sentence): A specific, genuine compliment or observation about their website or business. Reference something concrete you saw — a specific listing, their design, a client testimonial, their market positioning. NOT generic flattery.

2. PAIN POINT (1-2 sentences): Address the specific challenge you identified. Use phrases like "I noticed that..." or "Most agencies like yours spend X hours on...". Make it feel like you understand their daily reality.

3. VALUE PROPOSITION (2-3 sentences): Explain how our AI automation solution solves their specific problem. Be concrete:
   - Include a specific metric (e.g., "responds to new leads in under 60 seconds, 24/7")
   - Mention the technology naturally (e.g., "using an AI-powered workflow")
   - Focus on the OUTCOME, not the technology

4. CALL TO ACTION (1 sentence): Casual, low-pressure. Ask for a brief 10-minute call or offer to send a personalized demo video. NOT "schedule a meeting" — too corporate.

=== STRICT RULES ===

- NEVER start with "I hope this email finds you well" or any filler phrase
- Start directly with their company name or a specific observation
- Keep tone conversational and confident, not corporate or desperate
- ONE pain point only — don't list everything
- Include at least ONE specific number or metric in the value prop
- Write the email in English (the analysis can reference content in any language)
- Keep subject line under 50 characters, curiosity-driven, NO clickbait
